# Research design

## Objective

Evaluate how generative AI assistance relates to university student creativity and test the mediating roles of AI literacy and creative self-efficacy.

## Sample and instrument

- Online questionnaire administered in July 2026
- 519 valid responses from university students in China
- 36 measurement items
- Controls included gender, academic year, discipline, and prior GenAI experience

## Analysis

- Software: SmartPLS 4
- Method: Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM)
- Inference: 5,000-sample bootstrapping
- Checks: indicator reliability, internal consistency, convergent validity, discriminant validity, collinearity, common method bias, direct effects, indirect effects, and explained variance

## Model concepts

- Generative AI assistance
- AI literacy
- Creative self-efficacy
- Creativity

## Reproducibility boundary

The project documents the workflow and selected aggregate outputs. The unreleased manuscript, full abstract, item wording, raw responses, and complete model output are excluded.
