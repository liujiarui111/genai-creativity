# Generative AI Assistance and University Student Creativity

First-author quantitative research project accepted by the **2026 6th International Conference on Educational Technology (ICET 2026)** for oral presentation and publication.

## Research focus

This study examines how generative AI assistance is associated with university students' creativity, with AI literacy and creative self-efficacy considered as mediating mechanisms.

## Research pipeline

1. Designed and administered an online questionnaire.
2. Screened responses and prepared a dataset of 519 valid cases.
3. Assessed measurement quality and common method bias.
4. Estimated the structural model in SmartPLS 4 using PLS-SEM and 5,000 bootstrap samples.
5. Interpreted direct, indirect, and explanatory effects.
6. Converted the findings into a conference manuscript.

## My contribution

As first author, I worked across questionnaire design, data preparation, PLS-SEM analysis, interpretation, visualization, and manuscript preparation.
